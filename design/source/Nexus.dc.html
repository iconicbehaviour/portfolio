01 · The problem

A fast-growing consultancy with fragmented knowledge

Rapid growth and a shifting consulting model left Method's knowledge spread across tools, and every person organized it their own way. To find anything, people built workarounds or sifted through stores of files that weren't made to be searched.

Google Drive Slack Miro Figma Code bases
"I will find someone who knows before I look for what I need to know."
02 · Discovery

Who are Methodites, and how do they store, search and share?

We synthesized 400+ data points from interviews, observation and surveys into two personas and a user journey map.

What we found

Knowledge is dispersed

It lives in many tools, and none of them were built to be searched together.

People ask people

Without structure, the default is to find a colleague who knows before looking for the file.

Everyone files differently

Each person had their own system, so no shared way to organize or find work existed.

[Image: research synthesis board and user journey map]
03 · Opportunity

How might Method aggregate, organize and access its information?

04 · Co-design

Two workshops, two priorities

With six weeks left, we ran two co-design workshops with the client, end users and the wider team, building on the opportunity areas from research. Out of them we prioritized the two capabilities that would drive adoption and deliver value immediately.

Quick retrieval
Find the right file in minutes, not hours.
Fast uploads
Make contributing easy enough that people actually do it.
[Image: co-design workshop]
05 · My role as PM

Turning research into a buildable product

PRD and taxonomy

Wrote the PRD, including a content taxonomy and metadata for 300+ client case studies, and the success metrics.

Permission-aware access

Defined role-based access so search only returns what each person is allowed to see.

Backlog and roadmap

Broke the work into epics, user stories and acceptance criteria in Jira, and planned a three-quarter roadmap.

Discovery

Ran stakeholder interviews and workshops, compared 15+ tools, and followed a Double Diamond process.

Persona-based onboarding

Shaped onboarding around each persona, so new users see relevant content from day one.

06 · The solution

A single source of truth, with AI where it helps

Nexus is a web app that brings Method's knowledge into one place, with smart filtering, AI-assisted uploads and a personalized experience.

[Screen: search and chat]

Three ways to find

Search by exact file name, chat with AI using semantic search with citations, or browse the library.

[Screen: tags and filters]

Standard tags and filters

Filter by Method's own tags: content type, project phase, client and project name.

[Screen: smart upload]

Smart uploads

Upload from Drive, Figma, Miro or code, and AI suggests labels so filing is quick and consistent.

[Screen: personalized onboarding]

A personalized start

New users share their role and needs, so even empty states show relevant content.

07 · Key decision

Balancing automation and autonomy

We spent real time on what role AI should play: streamlining uploads and offering flexible search, while keeping storage standardized and letting anyone contribute. We defined the what before the how, and held every AI touchpoint to three things:

Data integrity User autonomy Consistency
08 · Delivery

From wireframes to a working MVP

  1. Interaction flows and low-fidelity wireframes for early feedback
  2. A shared design system so four designers could work as one
  3. High-fidelity designs and engineering handoff
  4. Annotations, Jira tickets, bug fixes and user testing alongside engineering

Built on RAG with chunking and citations, Gemini, a vector database, Firebase, the Google Drive API and Apps Script. The MVP shipped within two sprints.

09 · Outcome

Hours of searching became minutes

Finding information went from 5–6 hours to under 3 minutes, saving the company 600+ hours each week. The project was recognized with Method's Share & Shine awards.
